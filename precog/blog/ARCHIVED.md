# Archived: `precog/blog/`

A Rails blog-engine prototype, superseded by the top-level [`blog/`](../blog/)
and by the maintained app in [`todo/`](../../todo/).

## Status

**Not built, not tested, not deployed, not maintained.** It has a committed
`Gemfile.lock`, but that lockfile was resolved for Ruby 1.9 and pins gems that
no longer compile on a supported toolchain, so it will fail `bundle install`.

## Do not start here

Use [`todo/`](../../todo/) for anything that must actually run.
